Hello. My name is

Max Messick

"Finding the balance between humanity and technology has never been more important."

I am an artist who loves video games and dogs, and I have spent my whole life in Central Ohio — growing up in Westerville and building my life across Columbus, Gahanna, and beyond.

I have always had a passion for computers, having built my own gaming systems and spent years maintaining and fine-tuning them. That hands-on curiosity, combined with over a decade of customer service experience, has shaped my approach to problem-solving: patient, thorough, and people-first.

I am newer to the world of coding and web design, but I bring to it everything I have learned — a love of craft, an eye for detail, and a genuine desire to create experiences that feel good to use.
